Title Sequence Recreation

Overview

Completed in 6 weeks, this project recreates the title sequence for Home Alone 2: Lost in New York (1992), following Kevin McCallister as he's separated from his family and left alone in New York City just before Christmas. I kept the simplicity of the original sequence while incorporating symbolic elements from the film.

Storyboard

Before animating, I planned out the sequence to work out pacing, composition, and how each scene would hand off to the next.
